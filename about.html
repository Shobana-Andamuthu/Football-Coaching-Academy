Our story

Kickstart began with ten children on a rented ground in Nungambakkam. Parents asked for more sessions, then for age-based groups and match play, and the academy grew into four programs with licensed coaches.

Today we coach more than 300 players. Our aim is simple: give every child good coaching, regular match experience and a safe, positive place to love the game.

Our journey

How the academy grew.

  1. 2012: First sessions

    Ten players and one volunteer coach.

  2. 2016: Age-based programs

    Four age groups introduced with set curricula.

  3. 2021: League football

    Academy and Elite squads join city leagues.

  4. 2025: Parent portal

    Online enrollment, attendance and fixtures.

  5. 2026: Girls’ football and goalkeeper school

    New specialist programs, holiday camps and 300+ players on the roll.
