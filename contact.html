FAQ

Questions parents ask

Quick answers before you book a trial.

What age can my child start?

From age 4. Our Little Kickers program is built for first-time players, and every program is grouped by age.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every child can join one free trial session. Book it from the Contact page and we will confirm the date.

What should my child bring?

Football boots or trainers, shin pads, a water bottle and weather-appropriate kit. Training bibs and balls are provided.

How do parents follow progress?

Through the parent dashboard: enroll children, check attendance and view match schedules in one place.

What if my child misses a session?

Sessions can be made up in another batch of the same age group within the term. Tell us through the dashboard or by phone.

Are coaches qualified and checked?

All coaches hold recognised football coaching licences, first-aid certificates and completed background checks.
