Our method

How we coach

Every session follows the same simple pattern: warm-up with the ball, a skill focus, small-sided games and a short cool-down. Children touch the ball far more than in a traditional drill-heavy session.

Skills first

Dribbling, passing and first touch every session.

Small groups

One coach for every eight players, at most.

Match play

Weekend matches and friendlies from age 7.

Progress reports

Clear updates for parents every term.
